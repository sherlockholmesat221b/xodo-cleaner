Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Your PDF is opened and cleaned right inside this browser tab. It never gets sent to a server — closing this page is the same as it never having existed anywhere but your device.

Will this affect my own notes or highlights?

No. Only the watermark Xodo itself adds is removed. Anything you drew, stamped, pasted, or linked stays exactly as you left it.

Why does my PDF app still mention a watermark?

Some apps cache that warning from when the file was first opened. Try closing and reopening the cleaned file, or importing it as a new file rather than continuing your old session.
